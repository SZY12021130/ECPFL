B. Overall Performance
Fig. 6 and Table II summarize convergence, test accuracy,
communicationvolume,andtrainingtimeacrossfourdatasets.
ECPFL demonstrates rapid, stable convergence, matching
dense baselines, confirming that its prefetching and sparsifi-
cation accelerate training without compromising convergence.
ECPFL attains final test accuracies comparable to or exceed-
ing FedAvg and ESAFL, due to homomorphic operations
preserving numerical precision. For example, union-based
ECPFL achieves 82.77% accuracy on CIFAR-10, outperform-
ing FedAvg’s 64.41%. FedAvg, despite maintaining model
utility, transmits uncompressed models, resulting in 52.15
GB communication and 2750 seconds on CIFAR-10. ESAFL
reduces this to 26.46 GB but remains inefficient. ECPFL
further surpasses other sparsification frameworks: FedPHE
yields only 87.00% accuracy on FMNIST due to isolated
localsparsification,whileMASER’spartialdecryptionuploads
and global plaintext broadcasts inflate SVHN communication
to 71.02 GB and training time to 3956 seconds. ECPFL
addresses these inefficiencies via distributed consensus and
continuous ciphertext prefetching, which shifts downstream
payloadstoasynchronoustasks,cuttingsynchronousdownlink
overhead. The rank-based ECPFL variant, combined with
dynamic voting, achieves optimal efficiency, converging on
MNIST with only 0.80 GB and 80.31 seconds—over tenfold
communication reduction and fivefold speedup compared to
FedAvg, with a predictable upper bound on communication
payload.
