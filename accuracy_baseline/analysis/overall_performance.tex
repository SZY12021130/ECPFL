\subsection{Overall Performance}
\label{subsec:accuracy}

Fig.~\ref{Accuracy_compare} and Table~\ref{tab:comm-comparison} summarize convergence, test accuracy, communication volume, and training time across the four datasets.
ECPFL converges rapidly and stably, closely following the uncompressed FedAvg+CKKS baseline and confirming that prefetching and sparsification do not degrade convergence.
ECPFL-UN achieves 99.19\%, 91.20\%, 82.77\%, and 95.11\% on MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, CIFAR-10, and SVHN, respectively, remaining within 0.62\% of the uncompressed baseline.
On Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10, it outperforms FedPHE by 4.20\% and 7.00\%, respectively, as ESP consensus selects globally relevant gradient packs instead of isolated local top-k masks.

ECPFL-RK achieves the smallest total volume on MNIST and Fashion-MNIST, reducing communication by $10.7\times$ and $6.5\times$ and training time by $2.4\times$ and $3.0\times$ over FedAvg+CKKS.
ECPFL-TH achieves the smallest total volume on CIFAR-10 and SVHN.
On CIFAR-10, it reduces communication by $6.9\times$ and training time by $2.5\times$ over FedAvg+CKKS.
On SVHN, it reduces training time by $5.4\times$ over FedAvg+CKKS and by $5.7\times$ over MASER.
FedAvg+CKKS incurs the highest total volume among the single-key baselines, and MASER's partial-decryption uploads further inflate communication on SVHN.
ECPFL addresses these issues by combining distributed consensus with asynchronous ciphertext prefetching, moving the dominant downstream traffic into idle periods while preserving aggregation correctness.
