\subsection{Overhead and Efficiency Evaluation}
\label{subsec:communication}

\subsubsection{Communication overhead}

Figure~\ref{communication_bar} and Table~\ref{tab:perf_evaluation} together summarize the upstream and downstream communication volumes across methods, with Table~\ref{tab:perf_evaluation} reporting per-round batch counts and volumes and Figure~\ref{communication_bar} showing the 50-round totals. ECPFL-TH reduces total training communication by 4.0$\times$, 18.9$\times$, 7.0$\times$, and 4.8$\times$ compared with FedAvg+CKKS on MNIST, FMNIST, CIFAR-10, and SVHN, because it transfers only the consensus packs selected by ESP. The downstream reduction is larger: the synchronous fetch volume drops from 4.4 GB, 4.0 GB, 26.7 GB, and 45.8 GB under FedAvg+CKKS to 35 MB, 10 MB, 190 MB, and 299 MB under ECPFL-TH, corresponding to 124$\times$, 413$\times$, 140$\times$, and 154$\times$ reductions. ECPFL-TH also lowers total communication by 1.8$\times$, 9.5$\times$, 4.2$\times$, and 2.9$\times$ compared with FedPHE, since FedPHE clients must still download the full model update despite local upstream sparsification. ECPFL-RK achieves even lower synchronous downstream volumes of 0.3 MB, 0.4 MB, 7.7 MB, and 11.1 MB, corresponding to reductions of 292$\times$, 199$\times$, 69$\times$, and 59$\times$ over FedAvg+CKKS, by shifting most downstream traffic into asynchronous prefetching.

\subsubsection{End-to-End Training Time}

The reduction in synchronous communication is reflected in the end-to-end training time, measured as the total simulated wall-clock time over 50 rounds. ECPFL-RK completes MNIST, FMNIST, and SVHN in 129.9 s, 95.8 s, and 923.2 s, which are 2.4$\times$, 3.0$\times$, and 3.4$\times$ faster than FedAvg+CKKS, because prefetching removes the synchronous downstream bottleneck. The speedup is smaller on CIFAR-10, where ECPFL-RK takes 1441.4 s, only 1.2$\times$ faster than FedAvg+CKKS, since it uploads and aggregates 1175 and 47 batches per round, more than the 850 and 34 batches of FedPHE and ESAFL. ECPFL-TH shows a different pattern: while it is slower than the baselines on MNIST and FMNIST because the threshold consensus and sticky selection introduce overhead that outweighs the savings on small models, it finishes SVHN in 577.6 s, 5.4$\times$ faster than FedAvg+CKKS, since the large model makes the communication reduction from threshold-based consensus dominant. On CIFAR-10, ECPFL-TH takes 2756.7 s, slower than FedPHE and ESAFL, because the per-round upload and aggregation overhead remains high.
