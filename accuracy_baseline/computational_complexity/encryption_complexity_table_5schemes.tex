\caption{Per-round client-side \emph{encryption} complexity under a fixed

\label{tab:encryption-complexity}
\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Scheme} & \textbf{Packs enc.} & \textbf{Cost / pack} &
\textbf{Encryption complexity} \\
\midrule
FedAvg+CKKS  & $J$  & $\mathcal{O}(n\log n)$    &
$J\cdot\mathcal{O}(n\log n)=\mathcal{O}(d\log n)$ \\[3pt]
FedPHE       & $sJ$ & $\mathcal{O}(n\log n)$    &
$sJ\cdot\mathcal{O}(n\log n)=\mathcal{O}(sd\log n)$ \\[3pt]
ESAFL        & $J$  & $\mathcal{O}(n\log n)$    &
$J\cdot\mathcal{O}(n\log n)=\mathcal{O}(d\log n)$ \\[3pt]
MASER        & $J$  & $\mathcal{O}(n\log n+nL)$ &
$J\cdot\mathcal{O}(n\log n+nL)=\mathcal{O}\!\left(d(\log n+L)\right)$ \\[3pt]
\midrule
ECPFL (ours) & $1+k$ & $\mathcal{O}(n\log n)$ &
$(k{+}1)\cdot\mathcal{O}(n\log n)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}
\footnotesize
\emph{Notes.}
(i) $J$ follows each scheme's own packing: $J=\lceil 2d/n\rceil$ for the
slot-packed CKKS schemes (FedAvg+CKKS, FedPHE, MASER), $\lceil d/2n\rceil$
for ESAFL's EP bit-packing, and $\lceil d/n\rceil$ for ECPFL's coefficient
packing; in parameter units every $J$-term equals $\mathcal{O}(d\log n)$,
so the pack-unit comparison is also a comparison in $d$.
(ii) MASER's additive $nL$ term is the RNS-limb overhead of encrypting
under the aggregated multi-key public key; if only the majority-vote mask
subset ($m\le d$ parameters) is encrypted, replace $d$ by $m$.
(iii) ECPFL encrypts one vote ciphertext per round---the whole $J$-bit
ballot packed into the low coefficients of a single ring element---plus $k$
consensus-pack ciphertexts, hence $1+k$ packs. Deriving the effective key
$\tilde{s}_{i,j}$ (Eq.~11) additionally costs the share sums
$\sum_{k\notin\mathcal{S}}s_{k,i}$, i.e.\ $\mathcal{O}(Nn)$ coefficient
\emph{additions} paid once per round and cached across the $k$ packs; this
is not an encryption, it involves no ring multiplication, and with $N$
fixed it is $\mathcal{O}(n)$, hence absorbed by the
$\mathcal{O}(n\log n)$ per-pack term and omitted from the table.
(iv) On dropout rounds each surviving client additionally issues $k$
compensatory ciphertexts, so the total becomes
$(2k{+}1)\cdot\mathcal{O}(n\log n)$; the four baselines do
not tolerate dropout---FedAvg+CKKS loses the dropped updates while FedPHE
(uncancelled obfuscating factor), ESAFL (summed key $S=\sum_i s_i$) and
MASER (majority-vote mask consensus plus xMK-CKKS $\mathrm{PartialDec}$ by
every participant) cannot complete the round at all.
(v) Each ECPFL pack encryption performs two ring multiplications
($a_j\tilde{s}_{i,j}$ and $b_j z_i$) against one for the baselines, so
ECPFL undercuts full-model encryption only while $2k+1<J$, i.e.\
$\theta<\tfrac12$ under rank consensus $k=\lceil\theta J\rceil$.
\end{minipage}
\end{table}
