2) Datasets and Models: Fig. 5(a) illustrates our evaluation
of ECPFL across four diverse datasets [58], [59], [60] varying
in data scales, model parameters, and SIMD batch sizes:
• MNIST (LeNet, 11 packs): 10-class handwritten digits;
60,000 training and 10,000 test samples.
• Fashion-MNIST (FMNIST) (CNN, 10 packs): 10-class
grayscale clothing images; 60,000 training and 10,000
test samples.
• CIFAR-10 (ResNet-20, 67 packs): 10-class color images;
50,000 training and 10,000 test samples.
• SVHN (ResNet-32, 114 packs): real-world RGB digits;
73,257 training and 26,032 test samples.
In addition, we use the bandwidth measurements of [61],
collected from heterogeneous devices such as smartphones
and PCs. The lowest upload and download bandwidths on this
dataset are 0.03 Mbps and 0.01 Mbps, respectively. As shown
in Fig. 5(b), the median download bandwidth is 81.29 Mbps,
significantly higher than the median upload bandwidth of
18.42 Mbps.

packing.

tion.
