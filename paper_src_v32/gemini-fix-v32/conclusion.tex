\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper proposed \textsc{ECPFL}, a dropout-resilient ciphertext prefetching framework for cross-device federated learning that jointly optimizes upstream and downstream communication while preserving non-interactive one-step local decryption under client subsampling and upload-phase dropouts without peer-to-peer communication or decryption-share uploads. Upstream, \textsc{ESP} combines Shamir zero-sharing-based secure consensus voting with Lagrange-weighted dropout compensation to align sparse update packs across clients and reconstruct the exact aggregate encryption mask; downstream, \textsc{ECP} enables intermittently available clients to asynchronously prefetch and homomorphically accumulate sparse ciphertexts during idle periods for one-step local decryption upon rejoining. Extensive experiments across four benchmark datasets demonstrate that \textsc{ECPFL} maintains accuracy comparable to dense encrypted baselines while substantially reducing online communication overhead and accelerating end-to-end training. Future work will explore share-free, one-step aggregate decryption over standard dual-component ring learning with errors ciphertexts without cross-round noise accumulation across synchronous and asynchronous participation settings, as well as adaptive pack-level consensus mechanisms tailored to dynamic gradient distributions.
